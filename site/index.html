BUILT TO KEEP GOING

The process can stop.
The session stays.

With SQLite or Postgres storage, sessions and their event journals survive process restarts. Recovery policies determine how interrupted turns resume or fail. Your application reconnects to the session and its history.

* Memory-density basis. Akka.NET documents approximately 2.5 million actors per GB of heap. The headline refers to that lightweight actor foundation, rather than a measured capacity for complete Legate agents. Session state, conversation history, active model calls, tools, workspaces, and host overhead add memory; actual agent capacity depends on the workload. This is not a Legate benchmark or a claim of millions of simultaneous model calls.
