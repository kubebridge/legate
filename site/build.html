Proof

Tested like infrastructure

2189 tests in the core suite, store conformance across Postgres, SQLite and InMemory, wire golden files, Testcontainers proofs, and the live three-node kill smoke. Around 15 ms local cold start.

Extracted, not inventedLegate generalizes the BridgeMCP agent engine from a job model to a session model, with Docs/ARCHITECTURE.md as the normative target.
